THE VENUE

Not deep in the jungle. Not on the river either. Something else.

Jungle Project happens on a stretch of open ground about 3km outside central Vang Vieng, shaded by a few genuinely huge old trees. No boat, no long walk through rice fields — just an easy tuk tuk ride from town into a space that's been rigged with lights, sound, and enough room to actually move.

It's rougher and louder than a riverside sundowner. Strings of bulb lights through the branches, a booth built for a real system, and a crowd that shows up to dance, not to sit and watch the water.

What's actually happening

Five things that make a Jungle Project Friday different from a normal night out in Vang Vieng.

🎧

Live DJ sets

Back to back sets from 10PM to 4AM — no dead air, no long changeovers.

🔥

Fire performance

A fire show runs partway through the night — find a spot near the open ground to watch.

🎨

Face & body art

Artists are set up near the entrance most of the night — get painted before you find the dance floor.

🌳

Open ground, real trees

The whole thing runs outdoors under the canopy — no roof, no walls, plenty of air.

🚘

Free ride out

A free tuk tuk runs from town to the venue — you don't need your own transport to get there.
